<objective>
Delete pass 1's parallel hardcoded palette (`paper`/`ink`/`graphite`/`line`/`signal`) from the
codebase: rewrite all 107 utility-class occurrences across 9 files to their semantic equivalents,
then remove the five literal-hex color keys from `tailwind.config.js` (TOKEN-01).

bypasses them entirely. While it exists, editing `--background` or `--accent` in `src/index.css`

candidate direction as a values block — which is the entire premise of this phase and the next.
Plans `01-01` (dark values, font indirection) and `01-02` (direction switcher) build the plumbing;
this plan is what makes the plumbing actually reach the pixels.
