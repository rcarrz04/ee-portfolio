    from source. Resolve the path relative to the script via `__dirname` and `..`. Extract the
    `:root` block and the `.dark` block, and from each collect every declaration matching a custom
    property assigned three space-separated HSL components (hue number, saturation percent,
    lightness percent), tolerating decimals. Throw a descriptive error if either block is not found.

    Implement HSL-to-sRGB conversion, WCAG relative luminance, and the WCAG contrast-ratio formula
    (`(Llighter + 0.05) / (Ldarker + 0.05)`), rounded to two decimals. Print one `PASS`/`FAIL` line
    per assertion with the measured ratio and its threshold, and exit non-zero if any assertion fails.

    Assert, for the **dark** block, a minimum of 4.5:1 for each of these foreground-on-background
    pairs: `foreground` on `background`; `foreground` on `card`; `muted-foreground` on `background`;
    `muted-foreground` on `card`; `card-foreground` on `card`; `popover-foreground` on `popover`;
    `secondary-foreground` on `secondary`; `primary-foreground` on `primary`; `accent-foreground` on
    `accent`. Additionally assert `accent` on `background` at a minimum of 3:1 (large-text/UI threshold).

    Assert these **dark structural rules**, which are what distinguish an authored palette from an
    inversion: dark `background` lightness is at least 6% (not near-black); dark `foreground`
    lightness is at most 96% (not pure white); dark `card` lightness minus dark `background`
    lightness is at least 3 points (elevation exists); dark `border` lightness is greater than dark
    `background` lightness.

    Assert, for the **light** block, a regression-only baseline at the levels it already meets:
    `foreground` on `background` and `muted-foreground` on `background` at 4.5:1, `accent` on
    `background` at 3:1, `primary-foreground` on `primary` at 4.5:1.

    Finally, handle the known light-mode exception described in the `<known_finding>` section above:

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
